EHR组件的参与式设计：为IT专家与医院工作人员创造新型协作关系空间

Participatory Design of EHR Components: Crafting Novel Relational Spaces for IT Specialists and Hospital Staff to Cooperate

Louise Robert¹, Laurine Moniez¹, Quentin Luzurier², David Morquin¹ ACM CHI 20252025 CHI Conference on Human Factors in Computing Systems
CORE A* HCI顶会 ACM CHI 2025 Participatory Design

Abstract
English

Introduced in the early 2010s, Electronic Health Records (EHRs) are now ubiquitous in hospitals. Despite some undeniable benefits, they remain unpopular among health professionals and present numerous challenges. Studies have documented how EHRs lead to clerical burden, are insufficiently adapted to the reality of healthcare teams' work, and too often result in a loss of meaning and even burnout (Morquin 2019; Gardner et al. 2019; Tai-Seale et al. 2023). This phenomenon is a contributing factor to the ongoing human resources challenges in hospitals.

Our research, situated at the intersection of Health Information Systems studies, Computer Supported Collaborative Work (CSCW), Service Design, and Participatory Design (PD), investigates the transformative impact of involving users in developing new EHRs components within a dedicated space in the hospital. It involves collaboration between a large University Hospital, a University, and a leading European EHR vendor. The research team's interdisciplinary composition ensures a comprehensive perspective in addressing these challenges, comprising a clinician with a PhD in Information Systems Management, four IT Specialists, two Service Designers, and a PhD holder in Linguistics. We have developed two dashboards: Firstly, one that enables all team members involved in the care of patients under the Mental Health Act in psychiatry to coordinate their work (Iso4Psy). Secondly, one centered on individual patient antimicrobial stewardship decision support (AtbViz).

中文摘要

电子健康记录（EHR）自2010年代初引入以来已在医院中普及。尽管具有不可否认的优势，EHR在医疗专业人员中仍不受欢迎，并带来诸多挑战。研究表明EHR导致文书负担加重、未能充分适应医疗团队的实际工作情境，并常常导致意义感丧失甚至职业倦怠（Morquin 2019; Gardner et al. 2019; Tai-Seale et al. 2023）。这一现象是医院持续面临的人力资源挑战的重要原因之一。

本研究处于健康信息系统（Health Information Systems）、计算机支持的协同工作（CSCW）、服务设计（Service Design）与参与式设计（PD）的交叉领域，探讨在医院内专门空间中让用户参与开发新型EHR组件的变革性影响。研究涉及一所大型大学医院、一所大学以及一家欧洲领先的EHR供应商之间的合作。研究团队的跨学科组成确保了应对这些挑战的全面视角：包括一位拥有信息系统管理博士学位的临床医生、四位IT专家、两位服务设计师和一位语言学博士。团队开发了两个仪表板：一是Iso4Psy，用于精神卫生法案下精神科患者护理的团队协调；二是AtbViz，用于个体患者抗菌药物管理决策支持。

Crafting Novel Relational Spaces Through The Medium Of Design · 以设计为媒介创造新型关系空间
English

We are examining how integrated design practice can establish essential interaction spaces to overcome the historical challenges associated with the EHRs. Our research focuses on pinpointing the material and interactional elements that enable genuine participatory co-design (hereafter PCD, as used by Melles 2019), positively shifting power dynamics between IT specialists and users, and fostering a more balanced agency in their relationship.

We employ design tools to create relational areas between stakeholders, following the 'Telling, Making, Enacting' (TME) framework (Brandt, Binder, and Sanders 2013). This approach ensures that our participative design methodology is deeply rooted in practice. In the telling phase, our methodology includes over 100 hours of ethnographic observations and semi-structured interviews per case study as well as narrative workshops.

中文

本研究探讨整合性设计实践如何建立关键的交互空间，以克服EHR相关的长期挑战。我们着力于识别那些能够实现真正参与式协同设计（PCD，沿用Melles 2019的术语）的物质性和交互性要素，从而积极地改变IT专家与用户之间的权力动态，并在他们的关系中培养更加平衡的能动性（agency）。

我们运用设计工具在利益相关者之间创建关系空间，遵循"讲述、制作、enactment"（TME）框架（Brandt, Binder, and Sanders 2013）。该方法确保我们的参与式设计方法论深深植根于实践之中。在"讲述"阶段，我们的方法包括每项案例研究超过100小时的人种学观察和半结构化访谈，以及叙事工作坊。

The TME Framework: Telling, Making, Enacting · TME框架：讲述、制作与enactment
English

Fig 1. Novel relational spaces through design practice.

The Telling Phase (Fig 2): In the telling phase, our methodology includes over 100 hours of ethnographic observations and semi-structured interviews per case study as well as narrative workshops.

The Making Phase (Fig 3): The making phase includes co-design workshops for dashboards development, leading to the production of approximately 100 paper-based prototypes for the two use cases.

The Enacting Phase (Fig 4): In the enacting phase, our evaluation protocol is collaboratively developed and includes the joint creation of scenarios for laboratory tests. This process entails considering the physical space, furniture, and props to authentically recreate situated conditions, encompassing temporal elements like task interruptions or time pressure. In this regard, the practice of service design draws a parallel with theater production (Penin and Tokinwise 2009).

Bridging Gaps In EHR Dialogue · 弥合EHR对话中的鸿沟
English

We contend with a lack of clarity surrounding participatory constructs within healthcare (Masterson et al. 2022), a situation that, we find, further deteriorates when attention shifts from patients to Information Systems users, who are rarely involved (Busse et al. 2023). Traditionally, there has been a discrepancy between the availability of medical staff and management for consultation, significantly impacting EHR design. Balka et al. (2018) observed that healthcare managers and system designers often overlook the intricacies and variations of clinical practice. Overturning this perspective is crucial in our project, extending beyond involving doctors to include a diverse array of users.

This approach, however, does not diminish the significance of the perspectives and ideas from the development teams. An essential aspect of service design process is precisely considering the distinctive experiences of both the recipients and the providers (Penin and Tokinwise 2009). When the focus of developing health information systems (HIS) shifts from being strictly confined to the R&D departments of EHR vendors to hospitals, the dynamics undergo a significant transformation. The hospital, as characterized by Balka et al. (2018), is an 'ambiguous territory' marked by complex power dynamics. For development teams it serves as a non-traditional work environment. Our project also strengthens their role by actively engaging them in conversations with users and co-facilitating workshops. Here, too, design practice rebalances power dynamics and agency within these relationships.

中文

我们面临医疗领域参与式建构缺乏清晰度的问题（Masterson et al. 2022），我们发现，当关注点从患者转向信息系统用户时，这种情况进一步恶化——后者很少被纳入参与（Busse et al. 2023）。传统上，医务人员和管理层可用于咨询的时间存在差异，这严重影响了EHR的设计。Balka等人（2018）观察到，医疗管理者和系统设计者常常忽视临床实践的复杂性和多样性。颠覆这一视角在我们的项目中至关重要——不仅要让医生参与，还要纳入多样化的用户群体。

然而，这种方法并不会削弱开发团队观点和想法的重要性。服务设计过程的一个关键方面恰恰在于同时考虑接受者和提供者的独特经验（Penin and Tokinwise 2009）。当健康信息系统（HIS）开发的焦点从严格局限于EHR供应商的研发部门转向医院时，动态关系发生了显著转变。Balka等人（2018）将医院描述为一个充满复杂权力动态的"模糊地带"（ambiguous territory）。对于开发团队而言，医院是一个非传统的工作环境。我们的项目还通过让开发团队积极参与与用户的对话并共同主持工作坊，强化了他们的角色。在此，设计实践同样重新平衡了这些关系中的权力动态和能动性。

Results & Conclusion · 结果与结论
English

Our research has successfully transitioned from initial paper prototypes to functional components. Thus, the practice of design, as it becomes progressively integrated into the hospital, induces significant changes. In line with Manzini's argument (2011), the term 'designing for service,' rather than 'designing services,' recognizes that what is being designed is not an end result, but rather a platform for action, with which diverse actors will engage over time. This perspective aligns with the necessity for systemic transformation within the development of HIS where research teams act as facilitators of multidisciplinary innovation via design practice (Minder and Heidemann Lassen 2018). Such a method is a crucial condition for designing an EHR that accurately meets the specific needs of medical professionals and their allies.

In the end, we aim to demonstrate our ability to genuinely renew the approaches used in the development of these software solutions that impact the entire healthcare system. However, the difficulties in establishing these collaborative spaces are numerous and encountered daily. Many competing practical pressures are at play. Creating effective interaction platforms between publishers, the various users, and researchers is a complex and challenging endeavor. It requires not only the alignment of different goals and timelines but also the nurturing of mutual understanding and trust (Metz et al. 2022).

中文

我们的研究已成功从初始纸质原型过渡到功能组件。随着设计实践逐步融入医院，它引发了显著的变革。与Manzini（2011）的论点一致，"为服务而设计"（designing for service）而非"设计服务"（designing services）这一表述认识到，所设计的不是最终结果，而是一个行动平台，多元行动者将随着时间的推移在此平台上参与互动。这一视角与HIS开发中系统性变革的需求相契合——研究团队通过设计实践充当多学科创新的促进者（Minder and Heidemann Lassen 2018）。这种方法是设计出准确满足医疗专业人员及其合作者特定需求的EHR的关键条件。

最终，我们旨在证明我们有能力真正革新影响整个医疗系统的这些软件解决方案的开发方法。然而，建立这些协作空间面临众多困难，且每天都在遇到。许多相互竞争的实践压力同时存在。在供应商、各类用户和研究人员之间创建有效的交互平台是一项复杂且极具挑战性的工作。这不仅需要协调不同的目标和时间表，还需要培养相互理解和信任（Metz et al. 2022）。

Key Takeaways · 核心发现
Finding 01

EHR虽在医院普及，但导致文书负担加重和职业倦怠，参与式设计通过改变IT专家与用户之间的权力动态来应对这一挑战。

Finding 02

TME框架（讲述-制作-enactment）将参与式设计根植于实践，确保设计不仅捕捉用户需求，还通过情境再现验证解决方案的可行性。

Finding 03

"为服务而设计"视角强调所设计的不是最终产品，而是行动平台，要求研究团队作为多学科创新的长期促进者。

Finding 04

在医院这一"模糊地带"中构建IT专家与医疗人员的协作空间，需协调竞争性目标与时间表，并持续培育跨学科信任（Metz et al. 2022）。

整合患者、医疗专业人员、系统开发者与学者视角的数字信息工具共同设计

Integrating Perspectives of Patients, Healthcare Professionals, System Developers and Academics in the Co-Design of a Digital Information Tool

Grynne, Browall, Fristedt, Ahlberg, Smith PLOS ONE2021 DOI: 10.1371/journal.pone.0253448

Abstract · 摘要
English

Background: Patients diagnosed with cancer who are due to commence radiotherapy, often, despite the provision of a considerable amount of information, report a range of unmet information needs about the treatment process. Factors such as inadequate provision of information, or the stressful situation of having to deal with information about unfamiliar things, may influence the patient's ability to comprehend the information. There is a need to further advance the format in which such information is presented. The composition of information should be tailored according to the patient's individual needs and style of learning.

Method and findings: The PD methodology is frequently used when a technology designed artefact is the desired result of the process. This research is descriptive of its kind and provides a transparent description of the co-design process used to develop an innovative digital information tool employing PD methodology where several stakeholders participated as co-designers. Involving different stakeholders in the process in line with recommended PD activities enabled us to develop a digital information tool that has the potential to be relevant and user-friendly for the ultimate consumer.

Conclusions: Facilitating collaboration, structured PD activities can help researchers, healthcare professionals and patients to co-design patient information that meets the end users' needs. Furthermore, it can enhance the rigor of the process, ensure the relevance of the information, and finally have a potential to employ a positive effect on the reach of the related digital information tool.

中文

背景：被诊断患有癌症且即将开始放疗的患者，尽管已提供了大量信息，但仍经常报告有关治疗过程的一系列未满足的信息需求。信息提供不足等因素，或不得不处理有关不熟悉事物信息所带来的压力情境，可能会影响患者理解信息的能力。需要进一步完善此类信息的呈现格式。信息内容应根据患者的个体需求和学习风格进行量身定制。

方法与发现：当期望获得技术设计产品时，参与式设计（PD）方法论经常被使用。本研究属于描述性研究，提供了透明的共同设计过程描述，用于开发一种创新的数字信息工具，采用PD方法论，多名利益相关者作为共同设计者参与其中。按照推荐的PD活动邀请不同的利益相关者参与过程，使我们能够开发出对最终消费者具有潜在相关性和用户友好性的数字信息工具。

结论：通过促进协作，结构化的PD活动可以帮助研究人员、医疗专业人员和患者共同设计满足最终用户需求的患者信息。此外，它可以增强过程的严谨性，确保信息的相关性，并最终有可能对相关数字信息工具的推广产生积极影响。

Introduction · 引言
English

It is not uncommon for persons diagnosed with cancer to experience challenges in their care, and these may relate to the provision of information. Patients who have received some prior information about mediations related to their care tend to have more interactive discussions with the healthcare professionals, and have a more positive experience before, during and post-treatment. Further, when stakeholders are involved in developing their own information material, it tends to become more relevant and readable. Over 50% of all cancer patients are offered radiotherapy (RT) as a mode of treatment. Because the high-tech RT environment is non-accessible to the public, it is an unknown environment for most patients diagnosed with cancer about to undergo RT. There is an association between a lack of information and knowledge related to the RT treatment procedures and increased levels of anxiety and feelings of being unprepared at the beginning of treatment.

中文

被诊断患有癌症的人在护理过程中遇到挑战并不罕见，这些挑战可能与信息提供有关。收到过有关其护理相关调解信息的患者往往与医疗专业人员有更具互动性的讨论，并在治疗前、治疗中和治疗后有更积极的体验。此外，当利益相关者参与开发他们自己的信息材料时，信息往往会变得更加相关和可读。超过50%的癌症患者被提供放射治疗（RT）作为一种治疗方式。由于高科技RT环境不对公众开放，对大多数即将接受RT的癌症患者来说，这是一个未知的环境。缺乏与RT治疗程序相关的信息和知识与治疗开始时焦虑水平升高和感到准备不足之间存在关联。

Introduction — Patient Information Format · 引言：患者信息格式
English

The primary purpose of patient information is to prepare and support the patients' and their families' needs throughout the complex RT process. Providing accurate preparatory information to patients prior to RT will have a positive impact on patient-related outcomes and reduce misconceptions regarding treatment. The traditional format comprises the healthcare professional providing information face-to-face, often in the form of one-way communication within a clinical setting, commonly reinforced by providing paper handouts. Both the patient and the healthcare professional may have preconceptions of what type of information is needed and how it is best provided. The information provided to patients often outlines what the experts want the patients to know rather than considering what information the patients are able to comprehend. Despite the provision of a considerable amount of information, patients still report a range of unmet information needs about the treatment process and how the treatment may affect their relationships with their family. This may not so much relate to the amount of information provided but instead to the format and provision of the information.

中文

患者信息的主要目的是在整个复杂的RT过程中准备和支持患者及其家属的需求。在RT前向患者提供准确的准备信息将对患者相关结果产生积极影响，并减少有关治疗的误解。传统格式包括医疗专业人员面对面提供信息，通常以临床环境中的单向沟通形式进行，通常辅以纸质讲义。患者和医疗专业人员都可能对需要什么类型的信息以及如何最好地提供信息存在先入之见。提供给患者的信息通常概述了专家希望患者知道的内容，而不是考虑患者能够理解哪些信息。尽管提供了大量信息，患者仍然报告有关治疗过程以及治疗可能如何影响他们与家人关系的一系列未满足的信息需求。这可能与其提供的信息量关系不大，而更与信息格式和提供方式相关。

Introduction — Health Literacy & Information Tailoring · 引言：健康素养与信息定制
English

Several factors may influence the patient's ability to comprehend health information. The ability may be associated with inadequate provision of information, e.g., the way in which the information is presented may go beyond the reading comprehension level of many adults, or the patient is presented with a high load of information over a short period of time. This signifies that there is a need to further advance the format in which the information is presented.

There are a considerable number of initiatives providing diverse categories of information, however, few are specifically directed towards RT. The composition of information should be tailored according to the patient's individual needs and style of learning and involving patients as stakeholders can open new and more effective ways to ensure this. In this article, we describe the use of participatory design (PD) methodology in the development of a new digital information tool directed towards patients who have been diagnosed with cancer and are receiving RT treatment.

中文

多种因素可能影响患者理解健康信息的能力。这种能力可能与信息提供不足有关，例如，信息的呈现方式可能超出许多成年人的阅读理解水平，或者患者在短时间内被呈现大量信息负荷。这意味着需要进一步完善信息的呈现格式。

有大量项目提供不同类别的信息，然而，很少有专门针对RT的。信息内容应根据患者的个体需求和学习风格进行量身定制，将患者作为利益相关者参与可以开辟新的、更有效的方式来确保这一点。在本文中，我们描述了参与式设计（PD）方法论在开发一种面向被诊断为癌症并接受RT治疗患者的新型数字信息工具中的应用。

Developing Digital Information Technology with Stakeholders · 与利益相关者共同开发数字信息技术
English

Moving from involving patients and healthcare professionals as consultants in the later stages of the development of patient information material to engaging both groups together as active participants in co-designing patient information material is a relatively new concept that is not commonly explored. Active involvement of these groups as stakeholders in co-designing patient information material will provide unique insights into their experience and needs. Further, it is more likely to result in information material that has a higher relevance, which is preferred to information material produced solely by the clinical experts.

中文

从在患者信息材料开发的后期阶段让患者和医疗专业人员作为顾问参与，转向让这两组人作为共同设计患者信息材料的积极参与者，这是一个相对较新的概念，尚未被广泛探索。将这些群体作为利益相关者积极参与共同设计患者信息材料将提供对其体验和需求的独特洞察。此外，这更有可能产生相关性更高的信息材料，这比仅由临床专家制作的信息材料更受青睐。

Adapting Patient Information — Health Literacy · 适配患者信息——健康素养
English

Patients bring their own unique experience to a healthcare encounter, and the healthcare professionals bring their clinical knowledge and experience. The difficulties that patients have in assimilating generic patient information into their own personal and social contexts and experiences may be associated with low levels of health literacy (HL). In this article, HL involves the ability to obtain, understand, communicate, and act on information on health issues to promote and maintain health. Mårtensson and Hensing suggest that HL can be understood as a dynamic continuum. HL may be viewed as a context-dependent phenomenon that can be affected by the anxiety the patient experiences due to the stressful situation of having to deal with information about unfamiliar things. Adequate HL may be achieved in various ways. Patients have different approaches in how they can best adapt patient information to their situation; hence, there is a call for comprehensive information to be tailored in a format best suited to the individual. Adopting a person-centered approach, where the patient is viewed as a capable person beyond the diagnosis instead of placing the focus on the disease alone, will not only reduce misconceptions regarding RT but also increase the patient's ability to make an informed decision regarding their treatment plan.

中文

患者为医疗互动带来自身独特的经验，而医疗专业人员带来他们的临床知识和经验。患者在将通用患者信息融入其个人和社会背景及经验方面的困难可能与低水平的健康素养（HL）有关。在本文中，HL涉及获取、理解、沟通和就健康问题采取行动以促进和维持健康的能力。Mårtensson和Hensing提出HL可以理解为一种动态连续体。HL可以被视为一种情境依赖现象，可能受到患者因不得不处理不熟悉事物信息所带来的压力情境而体验到的焦虑的影响。充足的HL可以通过多种方式实现。患者有不同的方式来最好地将患者信息适应他们的情况；因此，呼吁将综合信息以最合适的格式量身定制给个体。采用以人为中心的方法，将患者视为超越诊断的有能力的人，而不是仅将焦点放在疾病本身，将不仅减少对RT的误解，还能增加患者就其治疗计划做出明智决定的能力。

Digital Patient Information and a Person-Centred Approach · 数字患者信息与以人为中心的方法
English

The emergence of digital technology provides innovative opportunities and can assist in providing person-centered information, enabling patients to make well-founded decisions. There are indications that patients prefer to be given information that they can access later for review at home and for sharing with others. Unlike traditional face-to-face information, digital technology presents a range of alternatives enabling the patient to select the format of information best suited to them and where they can apply the information in an iterative manner. Rapid advances in digital technology generate both opportunities and challenges in providing patient information and there is a strong call for digitalized tools to be used in healthcare. However, it is important that the development of digital tools is based on research that follows quality standards. Furthermore, rigorous research evaluating the validity and efficacy of such tools are scarce. Digital technology can, in an innovative graphical way, aid the preparatory information by enabling the patient to experience something previously unknown, in our case, the highly-technological RT treatment room, presented in 3D by applying Virtual Reality (VR)-glasses, or watching animated films in an information app.

中文

数字技术的出现提供了创新的机会，可以协助提供以人为中心的信息，使患者能够做出有充分依据的决定。有迹象表明，患者更喜欢能够以后在家查看和与他人分享的信息。与传统的面对面信息不同，数字技术提供了一系列替代方案，使患者能够选择最适合他们的信息格式，并可以以迭代方式应用信息。数字技术的快速发展在为患者提供信息方面既带来了机遇也带来了挑战，人们强烈呼吁在医疗中使用数字化工具。然而，重要的是数字工具的开发应基于遵循质量标准的研究。此外，评估此类工具有效性和功效的严谨研究仍然稀缺。数字技术可以以创新的图形方式帮助准备信息，使患者能够体验以前未知的事物，在我们的案例中，即通过应用虚拟现实（VR）眼镜以3D形式呈现的高科技RT治疗室，或在信息应用中观看动画影片。

VR Technology and Digital Tool Considerations · VR技术与数字工具考量
English

Employing VR technology presents an inherent potential in being interactive because its content is generated in real time, not ahead of time, which makes the experience even more immersive. The VR-technique allows patients a simulated experience of viewing the environment prior to their first visit to the RT department. Furthermore, digital technology enables the patient to have a simulated experience as well as access the information for review in the comfort of their home. It also allows the distribution and sharing of information with their family and friends. Although digital technology is depicted as "empowering" for both patients and staff, there are challenges that need to be considered to avoid a digital divide, as some patients, particularly those age 75 years and older, still lag behind younger persons in its adoption. e-Health literacy is defined in similar terms as HL, in that it refers to the ability to seek and appraise health information from electronic sources and to apply the gained knowledge to address health problems. This insecurity may hinder patients from being confident enough to use a digital information tool involving mobile devices.

中文

运用VR技术具有交互性的内在潜力，因为其内容是实时生成的，而非预先制作，这使得体验更加身临其境。VR技术允许患者在首次访问RT部门之前获得查看环境的模拟体验。此外，数字技术使患者能够在家中舒适地获得模拟体验以及访问信息进行复习。它还允许与家人和朋友分发和共享信息。尽管数字技术被描述为患者和工作人员"赋能"的工具，但需要考虑挑战以避免数字鸿沟，因为一些患者，特别是75岁及以上的患者，在其采用方面仍然落后于年轻人。电子健康素养的定义与HL类似，指从电子来源搜索和评估健康信息并将获得的知识应用于解决健康问题的能力。这种不安全感可能会阻碍患者有足够的信心使用涉及移动设备的数字信息工具。

VR Interventions and Research Gap · VR干预措施与研究空白
English

While VR is commonly used for distractive interventions to relieve pain and distress during medical procedures, there is a growing interest in the use of VR-interventions in managing cancer-related symptoms. Furthermore, it has been found that VR-interventions in relation to patient information has a positive effect on understanding of the RT process while reducing anxiety. While digital technology is increasingly integrated into clinical services, it is not known from research how the VR-technology can be tailored to present a convenient approach to meet each individual person's style of learning to adapt patient information related to RT-treatment. This article is part of a larger prospective Randomized Control Trial (RCT) study and constitutes a foundation and groundwork for the project. In this article, we aim to describe the PD process in developing a digital information tool with VR-technology to support persons diagnosed with cancer and increase their perception of being prepared for their planned RT treatment.

中文

虽然VR通常用于分散注意力的干预措施以缓解医疗过程中的疼痛和痛苦，但人们对使用VR干预措施管理癌症相关症状的兴趣日益增长。此外，已发现与患者信息相关的VR干预措施对理解RT过程具有积极影响，同时减少焦虑。尽管数字技术越来越多地整合到临床服务中，但研究尚不清楚VR技术如何量身定制，以提供一种便捷的方式来满足每个人学习风格，适应与RT治疗相关的患者信息。本文是更大型前瞻性随机对照试验（RCT）研究的一部分，构成了该项目的基础和根基。在本文中，我们旨在描述开发带有VR技术的数字信息工具的PD过程，以支持被诊断为癌症的人并增加他们对计划的RT治疗做好准备的感觉。

Method — Participatory Design · 方法——参与式设计
English

Under the umbrella of co-design, we followed the specific PD methodology developed by Spinuzzi. PD is an approach that seeks to actively involve all stakeholders in the design process and is often used when a technology designed artefact is the desired result of the process. With the ambition to create a digital information tool with VR-technology for persons diagnosed with cancer, we involved relevant patient groups as well as healthcare professionals as stakeholders in the process. Adopting a dynamic approach to HL alongside a person-centered approach, we aspired to produce a format of information, tailored in line with the patient's individual style of adapting information. Applying participatory activities enables the clinicians, researchers, and patients to co-design a tool that meets patients' needs. Ensuring that the digital information tool is relevant and user-friendly for the consumers, and that it is based on scientific evidence and experience-based knowledge, requires the involvement of several stakeholders.

中文

在共同设计的框架下，我们遵循了Spinuzzi开发的特定PD方法论。PD是一种力求让所有利益相关者积极参与设计过程的方法，当期望获得技术设计产品时经常被使用。怀着为癌症患者创建带有VR技术的数字信息工具的雄心，我们让相关患者群体以及医疗专业人员作为利益相关者参与其中。采用对HL的动态方法以及以人为中心的方法，我们希望能产生一种信息格式，根据患者个体适应信息的风格量身定制。应用参与活动使临床医生、研究人员和患者能够共同设计满足患者需求的工具。确保数字信息工具对消费者具有相关性和用户友好性，并且基于科学证据和经验知识，需要多名利益相关者的参与。

Setting, Participants and Procedure · 设置、参与者和程序
English

This project took place in a RT-department in a University hospital in Sweden. The project commenced 2017, and the pilot study was undertaken spring 2018. The design of this study is of descriptive origin. The study is part of a RCT approved by the Swedish Ethical Review Authority (DNR 2020-00170), and for the pilot study (DNR 917 17), written informed consent was obtained from the patients. For the different steps of the development process, the study was approved by the heads of the radiotherapy clinic; verbal consent was obtained from the participating members of staff and the Innovation Development Team through voluntary participation.

The PD methodology, which involves three stages: initial exploration of work, discovery process, and prototyping was, in relation to this article, combined with a protocol developed by Elwyn, which contains a variety of complementary steps in the work process. The development of the digital information tool emerged in the first four complementary steps, and reflections gathered in the co-design approach informed the development of the digital information tool in the last step (Step 5).

中文

本项目在瑞典一家大学医院的RT部门进行。项目于2017年开始，试点研究于2018年春季进行。本研究设计属于描述性起源。该研究是瑞典伦理审查委员会批准的RCT研究的一部分（DNR 2020-00170），对于试点研究（DNR 917 17），已获得患者的书面知情同意。对于开发过程的不同步骤，研究获得了放疗科主任的批准；参与的工作人员和创新发展团队成员通过自愿参与获得口头同意。

PD方法论涉及三个阶段：初始工作探索、发现过程和原型制作，在本文中，与Elwyn开发的方案相结合，该方案包含工作流程中的各种补充步骤。数字信息工具的开发出现在前四个补充步骤中，在共同设计方法中收集的反思为最后一步（步骤5）中数字信息工具的开发提供了信息。

Project Management Group & Stakeholders · 项目管理组与利益相关者
English

A project management group was formed, consisting of three researchers: one with previous experience of PD (FS), one with experience of the RT-process (KA), and one with experience of the cancer care process (MB). A professional innovation development team (IDT) i.e., system developers with expertise in gamification within healthcare, were involved and active throughout the process of developing the digital information tool. A team member of the IDT had personal experience of a cancer diagnosis and RT treatment and was therefore able to take on an additional role within the project to act as a valued patient representative through the process.

成立了一个项目管理组，由三名研究人员组成：一名有PD先前经验（FS），一名有RT流程经验（KA），一名有癌症护理流程经验（MB）。专业的创新发展团队（IDT），即在医疗保健中游戏化方面具有专长的系统开发者，在整个数字信息工具开发过程中参与其中并发挥积极作用。在初始需求评估中（步骤1），医疗专业人员、行政人员和正在接受RT的癌症患者作为利益相关者参与。对于步骤2和3，邀请了包括注册护士和管理者在内的顾问组来审查文本的准确性。IDT的一名团队成员有癌症诊断和RT治疗的个人经历，因此能够在项目中承担额外角色，在整个过程中担任重要的患者代表。

Initial Exploration of Work (Step 1) · 初始工作探索（步骤1）
English

One of the researchers in the project management group (KA) had a role as clinical associate professor at the RT department. Together with the manager at the RT department, KA was able to do a strategic selection among the staff to ensure a broad sample of professionals participating in the workshop. Prototyping (Steps 4-5) · 原型制作（步骤4-5）

Discussion — Person-Centered Approach & the 3 R's · 讨论：以人为中心的方法与3R原则
English

By applying a person-centered approach, the project management group was encouraged to incorporate different dimensions within the tool to enable the patient to use it in line with their personal style of learning. The approach also included the families involved, as the patient effortlessly was able to share the digital tool with others. Some patients in the pilot study experienced a greater understanding and support from the family as they by using the digital tool gained more of an insight of living with cancer and RT.

Engaging different stakeholders in the research process can improve the rigor, relevance, and reach of science (the 3 R's). Employing co-design, where involved stakeholders encouraged the researchers to broaden their understanding to look beyond traditional ways of producing patient information, enhanced the rigor of the process. Developing a high-quality digital information tool with different stakeholders, working in iterative steps throughout the process, is labor-intensive compared to more traditional approaches. However, involving healthcare professionals and patients, who are the end users, from the onset of the project ensured the relevance of the project. Involving patients and healthcare professionals in the co-design process and working with the healthcare professionals in the implementation process supports a wide reach of the digital information tool.

中文

通过应用以人为中心的方法，项目管理组被鼓励在工具中融入不同的维度，使患者能够按照其个人学习风格使用它。该方法还包括了所涉家庭，因为患者能够毫不费力地与他人分享数字工具。试点研究中的一些患者从家庭获得了更大的理解和支持，因为他们通过使用数字工具对癌症和RT生活有了更多了解。

Discussion — Time Investment, Tool Usage & VR Considerations · 讨论：时间投入、工具使用与VR考量
English

Consistent with previous studies, we experienced that applying a co-design approach can be time-consuming. However, the consequences of not involving stakeholders and not testing the digital information tool in a pilot study must be considered, as this may have a negative impact on the quality and usability of the end-product.

The positive effects of this innovative mode of providing information became apparent in the pilot study, where several participants highlighted the ability to revisit the information and the benefit of having a visual orientation of the RT-department. This led us to believe that there is a potential for the digital information tool to complement the one-to-one information clinical meetings as well as being a measure to improve HL and eHealth literacy.

The mobile app containing information was utilized to a greater extent than the app with the VR-glasses. This was interesting, as, throughout the development process, the project management group and the IDT had believed the VR-glasses to be the most useful component. This reiterates the importance of involving end users in co-design as well as testing the acceptability and feasibility of the tool in a pilot study.

There is a paucity in the literature reporting the adverse effects of VR-based interventions, however, caution should be observed, as VR is not without complications. From the pilot study, it became clear that motion sickness was a common VR-related symptom. This resulted in the team creating a link in version 2.0 enabling the patient to view 3D images directly on the mobile phone screen as a substitute to the VR-glasses.

中文

与以往研究一致，我们体验到应用共同设计方法可能很耗时。然而，必须考虑不涉及利益相关者且不在试点研究中测试数字信息工具后果，因为这可能对最终产品的质量和可用性产生负面影响。

Key Takeaways · 关键要点
English

Core Contributions

1. PD methodology for digital health tool development: The study provides a transparent, step-by-step description of how participatory design can be applied to develop a digital information tool with VR technology for cancer patients undergoing radiotherapy.

2. Multi-stakeholder involvement is essential: Involving patients, healthcare professionals, and system developers (IDT) as co-designers ensures the tool addresses real needs and is user-friendly. The 3 R's (rigor, relevance, reach) are enhanced through stakeholder engagement.

3. Pilot testing reveals unexpected findings: The information app was used more frequently than the VR app, contrary to the development team's expectations. Motion sickness from VR glasses was a common issue, leading to the creation of a non-immersive alternative.

4. Person-centered information delivery: Digital tools enable patients to access, review, and share information at their own pace, improving health literacy and reducing anxiety about unfamiliar treatment environments.

5. Bottom-up implementation: Involving healthcare professionals in co-design and implementation facilitates successful adoption, as opposed to top-down technology imposition.

Limitations and Future Work

6. Patient recruitment challenges: The study was unable to recruit patients for initial workshops, relying instead on surveys and individual briefings. The patient representative was also an IDT member, introducing potential bias.

7. Time-intensive process: Co-design is labor-intensive and time-consuming compared to traditional approaches, but the investment yields higher quality, more relevant, and more widely adopted tools.

8. Next steps: Version 2.0 will be formally tested in a full RCT with robust design and sample size to evaluate feasibility and usability.

中文

核心贡献

1. PD方法论用于数字健康工具开发：研究提供了透明的、逐步的描述，展示了如何将参与式设计应用于开发带有VR技术的数字信息工具，用于接受放疗的癌症患者。

2. 多方利益相关者参与至关重要：让患者、医疗专业人员和系统开发者（IDT）作为共同设计者确保工具解决真实需求并具有用户友好性。通过利益相关者参与增强了3R原则（严谨性、相关性、推广性）。

3. 试点测试揭示意外发现：信息应用比VR应用使用更频繁，与开发团队的预期相反。VR眼镜引起的晕动症是常见问题，导致创建了非沉浸式替代方案。

4. 以人为中心的信息传递：数字工具使患者能够以自己的节奏访问、复习和分享信息，提高健康素养并减少对陌生治疗环境的焦虑。

5. 自下而上的实施：让医疗专业人员参与共同设计和实施促进成功采用，与自上而下的技术强加形成对比。

局限性与未来工作

6. 患者招募挑战：研究未能成功招募患者参加初始工作坊，转而依赖调查和个人简报。患者代表也是IDT成员，引入了潜在偏倚。

7. 耗时过程：与传统方法相比，共同设计劳动密集型且耗时，但投资产生更高质量、更相关和更广泛采用的工具。

8. 后续步骤：2.0版本将在具有稳健设计和样本量的完整RCT中正式测试，以评估可行性和可用性。
